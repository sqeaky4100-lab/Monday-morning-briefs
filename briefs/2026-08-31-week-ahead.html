Check These Out

  1. GASB 103 — what actually changes on the page Effective for fiscal years beginning after June 15, 2025, so it lands on June 30 year-ends now and works its way toward the September-30 governments after. The interesting part for anyone who has just spent a week inside an MD&A: it kills “extraordinary” and “special” items, mandates five specific MD&A sections written as analysis rather than boilerplate, and requires dual variance reporting in the budgetary comparison — original-to-final and final-to-actual, with explanations for the significant ones.
  2. xypha’s AHK v2 script collection A single showcase script plus standalone pieces, all written for v2 rather than ported from v1. Worth a browse for the text-case converter (lower / UPPER / Sentence / Title / INVERT on selected text), the pixel-by-pixel numpad mouse nudge for clicking tiny spreadsheet targets, and MultiClip — a 25-slot clipboard manager that persists across restarts, which is a different animal from Win+V.

Tips

Excel’s Inquire add-in will diff two workbooks cell by cell

Useful when a review round hands you a “same file, few changes” pair — exactly the 25d situation. Turn it on once at File › Options › Add-ins, choose COM Add-ins in the Manage box, Go, then tick Inquire. An Inquire tab appears; open both workbooks and hit Compare Files. It reports changed values, changed formulas, and changed formatting in separate colours, which finds a moved rounding plug faster than eyes do. It ships with Office Professional Plus and Microsoft 365 Apps for enterprise.

Chrome’s address bar can search a single site

Visit a site with a search box once — Drive, Todoist, the GASB site — and Chrome usually records its search engine. After that, type enough of the domain to match, press Tab, and the omnibox switches to searching inside that site only. If a site did not register, add it by hand at chrome://settings/searchEngines with a short keyword and a URL containing %s. Two keystrokes replaces loading a page and then finding its search field.
