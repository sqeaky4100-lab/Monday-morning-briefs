Tips

  1. Excel finds row-level mismatches with one keystroke

    Select two adjacent columns you are tying out — a client figure beside yours — and press Ctrl+\ (Go To Special, Row differences). Excel selects only the cells in each row that differ from the leftmost column, so a fill color applied right after leaves every break highlighted and everything that ties untouched. Ctrl+Shift+\ does the same across columns.

  2. Chrome can search a site straight from the address bar

    Type the start of a site you use often, such as drive.google.com, then press Tab — the bar switches to searching inside that site and your next keystrokes go to its own search. Chrome builds these automatically from sites you use; they live under Settings, Search engine, Site search, where the shortcut can be shortened to a single letter.
